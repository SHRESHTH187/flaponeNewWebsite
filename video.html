About Us

Built By Pilots, Engineers & Compliance Specialists

FlapOne started in 2019 as a two-person Remote Pilot Training Organisation in Delhi. Today we're a DGCA-approved, ISO-certified drone technology company spanning training, enterprise operations, contract manufacturing and nationwide parts supply — built because most vendors could only do one of those things well.

We're still run by the people who fly, build and certify the aircraft, not a layer of account managers between you and the work.
